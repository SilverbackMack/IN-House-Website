Who we support

  • Young people aged 16–17
  • Care leavers and young people at risk of homelessness
  • Young people with mild to moderate learning needs
  • Placements of 6–24 months
  • Emergency placements, depending on risk, staffing and the home

What we can't accept

  • Young people who need specialist residential care
  • A history of arson

Not sure if a young person is a good match? Call us. We'll talk it through honestly.

From first call to moving in.

  1. 1Initial contactCall or email the Registered Manager.
  2. 2Referral packWe request the referral form and supporting documents.
  3. 3Assessment and matchingSafety, compatibility and support levels.
  4. 4Pre-placement visitFor the young person and professionals, where possible.
  5. 5DecisionWe confirm, agree a start date and prepare the home.
  6. 6AdmissionInduction, Young Person's Guide, support plan and keyworker.

What to send us

Full, accurate information helps us make a safe decision quickly.

  • Social worker assessment
  • Risk assessment
  • Pathway plan
  • EHCP, if applicable
  • Behavioural history
  • Safeguarding information
  • Placement objectives
  • Professionals' contact details
  • Confirmation of funding
  • Agreed communication expectations

Send an enquiry

Tell us about the placement you need and we'll call you back. Please don't include the young person's name or personal details here. We'll ask for the full referral securely.
