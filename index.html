Our approach

Every young person has strengths. We start there.

Our Solution-Focused Brief Therapy approach is positive, respectful and strengths-based. It's built into every keywork session, routine and skill-building activity, and every member of staff is trained in it.

We keep homes small and family-style, because stability and trust are what let young people grow, heal and move on with confidence.

The Independence Pathway

Support today. Skills for tomorrow.

The In House Independence Pathway: six skill areas, regular assessments, monthly reports to social workers and up to three months of outreach after move-on.

Home

Cooking, cleaning, laundry, routines, home safety and shared living.

Money

Budgeting, banking, bills, saving and financial responsibility.

Health

Appointments, medication routines, self-care and wellbeing.

Community

Travel, communication, personal safety and local services.

Tenancy

Rights, responsibilities, property care and move-on preparation.

Future

Education, training, employment, CVs, interviews and goals.

We support care leavers and young people at risk of homelessness, including mild to moderate learning needs, for 6–24 months. We cannot accept referrals needing specialist residential care or with a history of arson.
