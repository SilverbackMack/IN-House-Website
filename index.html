Every young person has strengths. We start there.

Our Solution-Focused Brief Therapy approach is positive, respectful and strengths-based. It's built into every keywork session, routine and skill-building activity, and every member of staff is trained in it.

We keep homes small and family-style, because stability and trust are what let young people grow, heal and move on with confidence.

Support today. Skills for tomorrow.

The In House Independence Pathway: six skill areas, regular assessments, monthly reports to social workers and up to three months of outreach after move-on.

HomeCooking, cleaning, laundry, routines, home safety, shared living
MoneyBudgeting, banking, bills, saving, financial responsibility
HealthAppointments, medication routines, self-care, wellbeing
CommunityTravel, communication, staying safe, local services
TenancyRights, responsibilities, property care, move-on preparation
FutureEducation, training, employment, CVs, interviews, goals

We respond within 24 hours, and the same working day for urgent placements. We support care leavers and young people at risk of homelessness, including mild to moderate learning needs, for 6–24 months. We cannot accept referrals needing specialist residential care or with a history of arson.
