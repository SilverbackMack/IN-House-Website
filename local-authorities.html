Working with us

We welcome spot purchase placements and are happy to discuss block arrangements. Pricing is tailored to each young person's assessed needs and is available on request.

  • A placement agreement signed with each placing authority
  • Fees payable two weeks in arrears unless otherwise agreed
  • Fees reviewed if a young person's support needs change
  • We can administer allowances on behalf of the local authority
  • Additional 1:1 support hours agreed to meet assessed need
