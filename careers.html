Careers at In House Supported Accommodation Ltd

Be the adult they remember.

Small homes mean real relationships with young people, not just shifts. We back you with the training, supervision and support to do this work well.

How to apply

The kind of employer we are

What you get from us.

SFBT training

Formal Solution-Focused Brief Therapy training, with ongoing consultancy.

A proper induction

Comprehensive induction before you work alone, plus mandatory training in safeguarding, mental health, behaviour and fire safety.

Regular supervision

Space to reflect, plus annual appraisals and a plan for your development.

Backup when it matters

A 24-hour on-call rota, so you're never on your own with a difficult situation.

The work

Keyworking with 16 and 17 year olds.

You'll be a keyworker for young people aged 16–17, helping them build the skills and confidence to live independently.

  • Keywork sessions and independent living skills coaching
  • Emotional support and risk management
  • Working with social workers, health and education
  • Helping young people prepare to move on

Who we're looking for

We hire for character.

Experience helps, but if you're patient, reliable and believe every young person can grow, we'd like to hear from you.

  • Empathy and emotional intelligence
  • Calm, clear professional boundaries
  • A commitment to safeguarding
  • Good written records and communication
  • A full UK driving licence is useful
