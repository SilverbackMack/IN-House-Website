Careers at In House

Be the adult they remember.

Small homes mean real relationships with young people, not just shifts. We back you with the training, supervision and support to do this work well.

Register your interest

What you get from us

SFBT trainingFormal Solution-Focused Brief Therapy training, with ongoing consultancy.
A proper inductionComprehensive induction before you work alone, plus mandatory training in safeguarding, mental health, behaviour and fire safety.
Regular supervisionSpace to reflect, plus annual appraisals and a plan for your development.
Backup when it mattersA 24-hour on-call rota, so you're never on your own with a difficult situation.

The work

You'll be a keyworker for young people aged 16–17, helping them build the skills and confidence to live independently.

  • Keywork sessions and independent living skills coaching
  • Emotional support and risk management
  • Working with social workers, health and education
  • Helping young people prepare to move on

Who we're looking for

Experience helps, but we hire for character. If you're patient, reliable and believe every young person can grow, we'd like to hear from you.

  • Empathy and emotional intelligence
  • Calm, clear professional boundaries
  • A commitment to safeguarding
  • Good written records and communication
  • A full UK driving licence is useful
